9. **Sentinel-2 water check for 15 Sep (roadmap P2-7).** Built on 2 Oct 2026 and baked into r4 (`s2_crosscheck`; no new raster, no FPPS, no action class, nothing from product 4009). It counts water or saturated mud (MNDWI above 0) on the clear pixels of the 5 Sep and 15 Sep Sentinel-2 scenes inside the district. Measured: 61% of the district clear on 15 Sep (86% on 5 Sep); 36.8 km² on 15 Sep against 13.8 km² on 5 Sep; 25.3 km² new where both dates are clear; the model at 10:58 ICT has 18.0 km² in the same clear pixels (21.4 km² in the district) and overlaps the observed area with a ratio of 0.155. The page shows it on 15 Sep only, names both observations (VIIRS and Sentinel-2) and says that the larger observed area **is consistent with** water or saturated mud left after the river fell; it states no cause and, since the review of 2 Oct 2026, no land cover (see follow-up 10). The limitation "no ponding or storage after the river falls" is now in the manifest. The figures differ from the roadmap scout's (35.0, 9.6 and 21.8 km²; 59% clear) because of two rules, not because of the data: the scout counted only scene-classification classes 4, 5 and 6 as clear and left out ESA WorldCover water, while this bake drops only no data, cloud shadow, cloud and cirrus, as the roadmap row specifies, and uses the bake's own channel rule for permanent water. With the scout's rules the same code gives 34.9, 9.7 and 21.6 km² (59% clear), and the stricter-clear reading is published beside the main one (35.4 km²). **Rachmania to review the method** after the A3 freeze, in particular: (a) the clear rule, which keeps "unclassified" pixels (9.9 km² on 5 Sep, of which 3.8 km² read as wet); (b) the threshold of 0 (0.1 gives 33.2 km², 0.2 gives 30.3 km²); (c) computing the index at 20 m with the green band averaged; (d) the label "low" for the block's confidence. **Owners to decide:** (e) whether to add ESA WorldCover 2021 (CC BY 4.0, on disk, not an input today) so that the page can state the cropland share of the 15 Sep area, which an off-bake check puts at 88% (90% under the scout's rules); the words "on fields" rested on that unpublished check and were removed on 2 Oct 2026, so this is now only a question of whether to add the input; (f) whether a later model change may be tuned to this check: the manifest's rule says that any comparison used for tuning is relabelled calibration-informed, and the check is not presented as independent of the page's images, which were visible while the keyframes were set; (g) the declared generation times: this bake records 2 Oct 2026 14:19 ICT, taken from the machine clock converted to UTC+7, which is earlier than the 14:33 the previous bake of the same day declared; the earlier bakes of this wave appear to have recorded the machine's UTC+8 wall-clock time with a +07:00 label.
