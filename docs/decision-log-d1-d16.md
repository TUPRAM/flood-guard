7. **Capacity-aware shelter plan and what-if levels (roadmap P2-9 and C-1).** Built on 2 Oct 2026 and baked into r4 (`shelters.capacitated`, `shelters.robustness`); the existing coverage ranking and its numbers are unchanged. It is a T1 scenario: no score, no action class, no participation sweep (D8b) and no listed capacity from another source in any public file. Measured at the 3.5 m peak, of the 14,169 residents whose homes flood: the default plan's 8 sites have 7,580 within the walk and hold 495 (lower bound) to 1,057 (upper bound); the capacity-aware ranking's first 8 sites hold 1,805 to 2,441; all 95 eligible candidates together hold 2,673 to 4,149. **Rachmania to review the method**, in particular four choices made here: (a) one site order serves both bounds and it is ranked on the upper bound, so a site with no footprint can enter the ranking and is then counted as 0 in the lower bound; (b) the median that stands in for an unknown capacity uses every candidate of that kind with an estimate, eligible or not (schools 253, places of worship 76, government offices 81; community centres have none and take 80, the median of every estimate); (c) the ranking ends at 30 sites or when the next site adds less than 0.5% of demand, the coverage ranking's own rule; (d) the what-if levels are 2.5 m, 3.5 m and 4.0 m, and the robust core for k sites is the set of sites among the first k at every level. **Owners to decide** whether the page may keep naming the DDPM shelter list as the source of the planning overlay's listed capacities (only the source is named; none of its values is used or published). The Thai strings of the new blocks, and the two wording rules added with them (`shelter_directive`, and a Thai denial of return periods), await the Thai language check (H12).

9. **Sentinel-2 water check for 15 Sep (roadmap P2-7).** Built on 2 Oct 2026 and baked into r4 (`s2_crosscheck`; no new raster, no FPPS, no action class, nothing from product 4009). It counts water or saturated mud (MNDWI above 0) on the clear pixels of the 5 Sep and 15 Sep Sentinel-2 scenes inside the district. Measured: 61% of the district clear on 15 Sep (86% on 5 Sep); 36.8 km² on 15 Sep against 13.8 km² on 5 Sep; 25.3 km² new where both dates are clear; the model at 10:58 ICT has 18.0 km² in the same clear pixels (21.4 km² in the district) and overlaps the observed area with a ratio of 0.155. The page shows it on 15 Sep only, names both observations (VIIRS and Sentinel-2) and says that water or saturated mud standing on fields after the river fell **is consistent with** the larger observed area; it states no cause. The limitation "no ponding or storage after the river falls" is now in the manifest. The figures differ from the roadmap scout's (35.0, 9.6 and 21.8 km²; 59% clear) because of two rules, not because of the data: the scout counted only scene-classification classes 4, 5 and 6 as clear and left out ESA WorldCover water, while this bake drops only no data, cloud shadow, cloud and cirrus, as the roadmap row specifies, and uses the bake's own channel rule for permanent water. With the scout's rules the same code gives 34.9, 9.7 and 21.6 km² (59% clear), and the stricter-clear reading is published beside the main one (35.4 km²). **Rachmania to review the method** after the A3 freeze, in particular: (a) the clear rule, which keeps "unclassified" pixels (9.9 km² on 5 Sep, of which 3.8 km² read as wet); (b) the threshold of 0 (0.1 gives 33.2 km², 0.2 gives 30.3 km²); (c) computing the index at 20 m with the green band averaged; (d) the label "low" for the block's confidence. **Owners to decide:** (e) whether to add ESA WorldCover 2021 (CC BY 4.0, on disk, not an input today) so that the page can state the cropland share of the 15 Sep area, which an off-bake check puts at 88% (90% under the scout's rules); without it the words "on fields" rest on that unpublished check; (f) whether a later model change may be tuned to this check: the manifest's rule says that any comparison used for tuning is relabelled calibration-informed, and the check is not presented as independent of the page's images, which were visible while the keyframes were set; (g) the declared generation times: this bake records 2 Oct 2026 14:19 ICT, taken from the machine clock converted to UTC+7, which is earlier than the 14:33 the previous bake of the same day declared; the earlier bakes of this wave appear to have recorded the machine's UTC+8 wall-clock time with a +07:00 label. The Thai strings of the block await the Thai language check (H12).
