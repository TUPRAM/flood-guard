- What the practice replay may be said to show: how one flood of 2024, as reconstructed by a terrain model with low confidence, would have looked from one home, hour by hour, so that a household can practise its plan.
- What it may not be said to show: what happened at any real address; which roads were closed in 2024; where shelters are or were open; how many separate ways out a home has (the replay's road file is not a searchable network); anything about today. The practice reports on the map are invented exercise items and carry the "EX" tag.
