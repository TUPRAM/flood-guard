## Why a second note

Product 3991 is an analysis by UNOSAT, so UNOSAT is the direct source of its geometry. A dated extent with a
stated licence would be the first September reference the project may use. Until now product 3991 is used only
as a size check that informed the calibration of the replay (decision log R1).
