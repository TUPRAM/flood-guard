## Scope and handoff

Artwork generation, visual review and packaging are complete for this run. Website implementation is a separate step. Full-frame typography and UI are baked into these raster concepts. The clean plates and transparent portraits support later composition; exact text remains in the supplied specifications. No editable 3D model, layered design file, motion implementation, or accessible text layer is included.

Desktop originals are 1672 × 941; mobile originals are 853 × 1844. The supplied 1920 × 1080 and 390 × 844 figures are design targets. Original PNGs are preserved without artificial upscaling.

Visual continuity is reference-derived and was reviewed for landmarks, the road relationship, water progression, character identity, palette and labels. This does not establish exact pixel-registered geometry. Minor texture, foliage, framing and expression variation remains. The amber affected portion is longer than the short L01 example, matching the road portion shown under water. Both dry endpoints and the consequence remain clear. Human design acceptance and production integration are separate from concept review.

All geography and water states are fictional illustrations. These images are not current conditions, forecasts, measured reconstructions, or operational route recommendations. The creation date records artifact provenance only.
