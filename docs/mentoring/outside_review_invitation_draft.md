*[If the reader does not]* We are TeamBits, a finalist team of GeoHackathon
2026. Our project, FloodGuard Thailand, turns an existing flood map into
planning information about access: who is cut off, who loses the hospital,
and which roads matter.

We have a one-page summary of what the tool found for Mae Sai district in a
scenario built from the 2024 flood season, and we would value the view of

and not an official source; every figure is a scenario with modelled roads and
modelled residents, and nothing was checked on the ground. And we will record
your answer as the view of one reader, with your name only if you agree. We
will not describe it as an approval.

Thank you for considering it.
