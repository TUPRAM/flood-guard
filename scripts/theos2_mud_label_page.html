Mud or not mud: 150 points on the THEOS-2 image of Mae Sai, 17 September 2024

You will see one point at a time: a square of 2 m outlined in yellow, on three pictures of the same place, five days after the flood. Say what the square is. It takes about half an hour, and you can close the page and come back: your labels are kept in this browser.

MudMore than half of the square is a fresh deposit of the flood: brown or grey mud, silt or sand on the ground, on a road, in a yard or over a field, with no plants over it; also a film of mud on pavement.
Bare, cannot tellBare ground that could be flood mud or could be a tilled field, a building site or a dirt yard: you cannot tell which.
Not mudAnything else: plants, a roof of any colour, clean pavement, a shadow, and water. Brown water in a pond or a river, often with white sun glints, is water: not mud.
Cannot tellCloud, haze, a shadow too deep to see into, or no decision after looking at the three pictures.

Left: the 64 m around the point in true colour. Middle: the same in false colour, where plants are red and bare ground, sand and mud are pale green. Right: the 320 m around it. Label the square, not the surroundings.

Please label alone, do not look at any mud or water map of the project while you label, and do not compare single points with the other labellers until all three files are saved. The first five points are practice and are not kept.
